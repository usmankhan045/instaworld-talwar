# Talwar Organics brand research

The storefront direction was informed by a short review of Brightland, Graza,
Flamingo, and Canezo, followed by several desktop and mobile layout studies.
Generated screenshots and intermediate HTML previews are intentionally excluded
from this repository; they were visual QA output rather than source material.

## Findings kept from the research

- Lead with one large, product-focused editorial image and concise copy.
- Use warm cream, butter, sand, and coffee brown to support the natural jaggery
  photography. Avoid the blue direction tested during exploration.
- Present the two consumption rituals as distinct scenes: classic gur with chai
  and dry-fruit gur with green tea.
- Keep the product texture and irregular shape visible instead of making the
  food look overly polished or uniform.
- Use generous spacing and a simple two-product path; dense card grids weakened
  the story and made the small catalog feel repetitive.
- On mobile, place imagery before supporting copy and keep purchase actions easy
  to reach.
- Avoid unsupported health, sourcing, certification, and scarcity claims.

## Retained source material

Reusable photography, the wordmark, compact artwork, and original product
